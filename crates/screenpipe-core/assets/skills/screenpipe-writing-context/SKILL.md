# writing context

Resolve the writing topic, audience, format, and period. Use screenpipe-api to find relevant outcomes, examples, and the user's own prior phrasing. Select only evidence needed for this piece; unrelated private activity is out of scope.

Build the draft around supported facts and distinguish completed work, plans, opinions, and uncertain recollections. Verify quoted wording against the source. Preserve the user's stated voice preferences without copying other people's private messages as the user's words.

Remove customer identifiers, credentials, and internal metrics from public drafts unless their disclosure is authorized. Present the draft in chat unless another destination was requested. Writing a draft does not authorize sending, posting, scheduling, or inventing results.
