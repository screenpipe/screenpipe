# project status

Identify the project, audience, reporting period, and milestone or goal. Use screenpipe-api to find relevant work and decisions; verify important delivery claims against current artifacts or connected project records when available.

Summarize progress toward each stated milestone, completed outputs, blockers, and the next decision or action. Separate observed activity from delivery and user acceptance. Do not infer progress percentages, health scores, owners, or dates from activity volume.

Cite a small set of dated sources and state what could not be checked. Tailor detail to the audience and omit unrelated private work. Produce a draft unless publication or a specific destination update is authorized.
