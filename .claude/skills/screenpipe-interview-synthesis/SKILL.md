# interview synthesis

Identify the interview and research question. Use screenpipe-api to retrieve its note and bounded transcript. Check speaker identity and recording coverage before attributing statements or quoting.

Capture concrete past behavior: the situation, intended outcome, steps taken, current workaround, constraints, and consequences. Separate what the participant did from what they say they might do, interviewer suggestions, and your interpretation. A positive reaction is not adoption or purchase intent.

When comparing the interview with usage data, name the outcome the participant wanted and check whether the measured action demonstrates it. Retrieval or a generated draft alone does not prove that outcome. State missing coverage and look for completion in the requested destination when access is available.

Return a concise evidence-backed account with source pointers, conflicting statements, and the next questions to investigate. One participant does not establish prevalence. Use role labels in shareable drafts and preserve identifying quotes only when appropriate to the requested audience. For themes across multiple interviews, use screenpipe-research-synthesis.
