### Correction after checking the exact candidate source

[The search client](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/blob/92355d2219c5898d3e75a35472986f90185fd2e6/apps/screenpipe-app-tauri/lib/hooks/use-keyword-search-store.tsx#L337-L342)
subtracts one minute from the request time and rounds down to the minute before
sending `end_time`. With no explicit date filter, this excludes roughly the
newest 60–120 seconds. The comment identifies indexing lag as the reason.

In the video, the selected frame is timestamped 00:10:35 UTC and search occurs
around 00:10–00:11 UTC: that frame is outside the search window. Relaunching does
not remove this cutoff. The original test did not prove a matching older frame
had indexed text, nor preserve the authenticated keyword request/response.
Therefore this evidence cannot establish a product search regression. It also
cannot prove the cutoff is the sole cause of all empty results in the run.

A conclusive retest must retain a known captured page, wait more than two minutes,
issue a fresh search, and inspect indexed text and the actual request/response if
it remains empty. No product fix is included. At the final health check, screen
and audio status were both ok.
